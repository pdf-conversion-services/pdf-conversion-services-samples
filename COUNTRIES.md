# Country variants

The linked business website lists remote services for the United States, United Kingdom and Australia. A country variant reflects paper size, date labels and relevant address labels; it does not imply a local office, legal approval or country-specific certification.

| Market | Template choices | Suggested search wording |
|---|---|---|
| United States | Letter (8.5 x 11 in); MM/DD/YYYY; State and ZIP code where relevant | US / USA; document digitization |
| United Kingdom | A4 (210 x 297 mm); DD/MM/YYYY; County / region and postcode where relevant | UK; document digitisation |
| Australia | A4 (210 x 297 mm); DD/MM/YYYY; State / territory and postcode where relevant | Australia; document digitisation |

Country formats are practical defaults and can be changed. These templates are general business planning forms, not tax, immigration, medical or legal forms. No currency, tax rate or local compliance claim is embedded.

Coverage source checked on 15 October 2026: [website locations section](https://pdfserviceexperts.com/). Country-specific wording is suggested editorial targeting, not a verified search-volume or ranking claim.
